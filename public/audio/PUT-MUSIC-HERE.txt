Drop ONE audio file in here:  public/audio/

• .mp3 is the safest format (works everywhere). .m4a / .ogg / .wav also work.
• Then open src/config/site.config.js and set:
      music: { src: 'audio/your-file-name.mp3', ... }
• Keep the file under ~10 MB so the site stays quick to open on a phone.
• Use a song you have the rights to share. If you would rather not worry about
  that, leave music.src as '' — the site then plays a soft original ambient
  loop generated live in the browser (no copyright, no download).
